# Sample scenes: Teo's voice

**Status: VOICE APPROVED AS STARTING POINT (2026-10-18).** The voice is the reference for all writing. The individual lines are not final. Choices are marked `>`. Conditions are in [brackets]. Purpose: to judge the inner-monologue voice, and to show how mood, intoxication and memory change the same moment.

---

## 1. Tuesday, 06:47. Bedroom.

**[Starting mood: FLAT]**

The alarm goes off. I've been awake for twenty minutes, waiting for it. Like waiting for a bus I don't want to catch.

Tuesday. The summer party. Viktor put it on a Tuesday so nobody drinks.

Viktor has met us, hasn't he?

Through the wall, Stojan: "—because it's MY balcony!" A door slams. Then the daughter's music. Just bass, and a girl singing in English about being disappointed.

**[Starting mood: GOOD]** *(rare)*

I wake up before the alarm and feel fine. Rested. Suspiciously fine.

Something's going to go wrong today, and I'm going to deserve it.

> Get up.
> Check phone.
> Lie here a bit longer.

**Check phone**

Two messages.

Magda, office group: *PARTY TONITE!!! 🎉 dont forget gift money 500 each!!! see u all xx*

Gift for whom? We're the gift. We're giving ourselves 500 each.

Kornel, privately: *Good morning Teo. Would you like to share a taxi to the venue? I have calculated that it is cheaper.*

He's calculated it. Of course he has. Somewhere there's a spreadsheet with my name in it.

> Reply to Kornel: "Sure."
> Reply to Kornel: "Might go straight from the office."
> Don't reply.

*(Remembered either way. Kornel notices silence more than anyone.)*

**Lie here a bit longer**

Thirty-two. When my father was thirty-two he had a wife, two kids and a Lada.

I have a ficus.

The ficus is dying. We understand each other.

## 2. Tuesday, 07:30. Stairwell.

Stojan is on the landing in a vest, smoking under the sign that says *No Smoking in the Stairwell*. He put the sign up.

**Stojan:** Teo! Mr Permit!

Nobody calls me that. Stojan calls me that.

**Stojan:** Listen. Small thing. The balcony.

The "balcony" is a room now. It has a window, a roof and, since May, a toilet.

**Stojan:** People from your office came. Took pictures. Very rude. Very professional. You make this go away, yes? Neighbours.

> "I can't really do that, Stojan."
> "Who came? Do you know their names?"
> "I'll see what I can do."
> [GOOD mood or tipsy] "What's in it for me?"

**"What's in it for me?"**

Stojan laughs, a big one. He points his cigarette at the wall.

**Stojan:** You fix this, you can marry my daughter.

**Teo:** She's seventeen, Stojan.

**Stojan:** So you have one year to fix it. I'm a patient man.

Through the wall, something breaks. Stojan doesn't flinch.

He's a patient man.

**"I'll see what I can do."**

That sentence has never once meant what it says. Everyone in this country knows it. Stojan knows it.

Stojan smiles anyway. Stojan has decided to hear "yes."

*(Memory: Stojan believes Teo promised. He will bring it up. Possibly in front of people.)*

**"I can't really do that."**

**Stojan:** Of course. Of course. Rules.

He nods as if I've said something wise. Then he looks at my door for slightly too long, like a man memorising a door.

## 3. Same stairwell, Wednesday, 01:40.

*(The same place, played back after the party. What happens depends on how drunk Teo is and what he said in the morning.)*

**[Sober or tipsy] + [promised Stojan]**

Stojan is on the landing again. Same vest. Same cigarette. Possibly he lives here, on the landing.

**Stojan:** Mr Permit. So? You fixed?

It's two in the morning. He's asking me about a toilet.

> "Working on it."
> "Stojan, it's two in the morning."
> "I'm not going to fix it."

**[Drunk] + [promised Stojan]**

Stojan. Stojan on the landing. Stojan is a landing.

**Stojan:** Mr Permit! So? You fixed?

I love Stojan. Why have I never told him? I need to tell him.

> "I'll fix everything, Stojan. Everything."
> Hug Stojan.
> [Very drunk] Try your key in Stojan's door.

**[Very drunk] Try your key in Stojan's door**

The key doesn't fit. I try harder. Keys fit if you believe in them.

The door opens from the inside. It's the wife, in a dressing gown, holding a frying pan like she's done this before.

## Notes on the voice

- First person, present tense. Short sentences. He's honest with himself, mostly about other people.
- The jokes come from what he notices and doesn't say. The monologue is funnier than his dialogue.
- Drunk Teo is warmer, stupider and briefly happy. That should make the morning after hurt more.
- The darkness sits underneath ("the ficus is dying, we understand each other"), not on top.
- **On the daughter joke:** your version, where Stojan doubles down about her hips, puts the punchline on her body. In a game with adult content, a line about a 17-year-old's body is the kind of thing players and Steam reviewers screenshot. And it isn't the funnier version anyway. Here, the punchline is on **Stojan**: "I'm a patient man", followed by something breaking behind the wall. Teo's "she's seventeen" line stays exactly as you wanted.
