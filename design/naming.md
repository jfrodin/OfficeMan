# Naming proposal

**Status: ADOPTED PROVISIONALLY (2026-10-18).** The bold first picks are in use. Easy to change later.

## Principle

The country's language is invented, a blend of Slavic, Hungarian and Romanian sounds, so no real nation is the joke. Names should be:

- easy for English speakers to read and remember,
- clearly "from somewhere", but not from one specific real country,
- never jokes in themselves. The people are funny; their names aren't.

**Consistency rules** (so invented names feel like one language):

- Surnames use a few recurring endings: **-escu**, **-ák**, **-ovar**, **-ny**, **-ici**.
- Light diacritics only: **á, é, š, ž**. No unreadable consonant clusters.
- People often use short forms among friends: Teodor → Teo, Magdalena → Magda.
- Banned: Andrey, Vladimir, Ivan, Olga, and anything that immediately reads as "Russian villain."
