# Who Teo is: traits and conditions

**Status: PROPOSAL for the trait design session (2026-10-18).** Nothing here is decided.

## The idea in one line

Teo's personality is **four tensions, not four scores**. Each trait is a line between two poles, and *both ends are
useful and both ends hurt*. There is no "maxed out" Teo, only different men.

That's *No Optimal Life* applied to the character sheet. A bold Teo gets the dance, the kiss, the promotion offer, and
the humiliation, the fight and the sobering-up station. A cautious Teo notices Zdenka watching, keeps his job, and
goes home alone with the ficus.

## The four tensions (names are placeholders)

| Tension | One end | The other end | What it opens | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nerve** | **Forward:** says it, does it, steps up | **Back:** watches, waits, swallows it | Forward: jokes, flirting, confrontation, opportunities | Forward: humiliation, burned bridges. Back: missed chances, resentment, being overlooked |
| **Warmth** | **Open:** feels for people, gets involved | **Closed:** keeps his distance | Open: people confide, ask favours, fall for him | Open: gets used, entangled, carries other people's mess. Closed: safe, cold, alone |
| **Appetite** | **Hungry:** drink, sex, food, spending, now | **Held:** steady, sober, careful | Hungry: wild nights, intimacy, stories | Hungry: hangovers, regret, addiction over time. Held: a smaller life, regrets of omission |
| **Candour** | **Blunt:** says what he thinks | **Smooth:** says what people want to hear | Blunt: trust, respect from some (Zdenka) | Blunt: offence, enemies. Smooth: liked, promoted, complicit (Viktor's world) |

### How a tension works in play
- **Checks can favour either end.** "Make the joke at Viktor's speech" rewards Forward. "Notice that Zdenka saw you bury the file" rewards Back. Neither end is the good end.
- **Some choices only appear near one end.** A Back Teo rarely gets "What's in it for me?"; a Closed Teo isn't offered "Sit with her. Say nothing."
- **The same choice is phrased differently** depending on where he stands. Forward: *"Tell him he's wrong."* Back: *"Say something? You could say something."*

## Conditions (temporary)

Conditions push the tensions for a while. They're how one evening can make Teo someone he isn't.

| Condition | Comes from | Pushes |
|---|---|---|
| **Drunk** (0–4) | Drinking | Nerve forward, appetite hungry, candour blunt; he notices less |
| **Hungover** | Last night | Nerve back, warmth closed, everything slower |
| **Tired** | Hours awake | Warmth closed; he notices less |
| **Mood** (low … high) | The day so far | Low: back and closed. High: forward and open |
| **Shaken** | A humiliation | Nerve well back for the rest of the day |
| **Riding high** | A success | Nerve forward; careless |
| **Wanting** | Flirtation, closeness, being wanted | Appetite hungry; judgment about people blurs |

Example: a Back, Held Teo who drinks four zhivkas is, for one hour, a Forward, Hungry man. That's the story.

## Seeing without numbers

The player never sees a value. Teo's personality shows up as:

1. **His inner voice.** Key lines have variants by tension and condition. Drunk Teo is warm and stupid; shaken Teo talks to himself in short, ugly sentences.
2. **Which choices exist** and how they're worded (above).
3. **Other people.** "You always do that, Teo." "You're easy to talk to." "You never say anything."
4. **Hindsight.** After an important outcome, one line names the cause: *"That was the third zhivka talking."* *"I should have just kept my mouth shut. I never keep my mouth shut."* When luck decided it, he says so: *"Some days the railing holds."*
5. **The end of the day** describes who Teo was today, in words.

## Starting values: three options

| Option | How | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Fixed Teo** | The same leanings every life | A consistent, authored character; easiest to write | Replays differ only by choices and luck |
| **B. Small variation** *(recommended)* | Your authored baseline, with one or two tensions nudged a step either way per life | Every life starts from a slightly different man; still recognisably Teo | A little more writing for the variants that matter |
| **C. Shaped by the player** | The first morning's choices set the leanings | Player ownership | Feels like character creation; invites optimising |

## Change over a life

- **Within a day:** conditions only.
- **Lasting:** big moments drift a tension one step: the first time he stands up to Viktor, a night he can't remember, a humiliation in front of everyone. Drift is slow, rare, and always *caused by something the player saw*.
- **Over chapters:** these drifts are what make the Teo of chapter 3 a different man. A midlife crisis in chapter 2 could be one large, deliberate shift.

## Questions for the creator

1. **Tensions instead of scores:** does the idea work for you?
2. **Are these the right four?** Candidates to swap in: **Pride** (vanity, can't lose face), **Suspicion** (paranoid vs trusting), **Conscience** (guilt vs shamelessness). And should **desire** be separate from appetite, given how central sex is to the cast?
3. **Who is Teo at baseline?** Where does *your* Teo sit on each line before the player touches him? (My guess: slightly Back, Open, Held, Smooth: a man who's let life happen to him.)
4. **Starting values:** A, B or C?
5. **Names:** you decide what they're called, since they're part of the game's voice.
